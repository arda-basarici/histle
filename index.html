histle

Guess the hidden historical event. Every wrong guess tells you how far off you are — in years, continents, and kind.

      How to read a guess

      ▲ / ▼
      The answer happened after / before your guess.
      Years
      How far apart the two events are, as a band.
      ✓ ~ ✗ ?
      Region and category: match, near, miss, unknown.
      Border
      Overall closeness — green, amber, grey.
      0–100
      How close that guess was, read off the four cells beside it — 100 only for the answer.

      Guess as many times as you like — your guess count is the score. After six guesses you can buy a hint; each one costs two guesses. Pick every guess from the suggestion list.
